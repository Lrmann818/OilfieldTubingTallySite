Support

For support or bug reports, contact support@oilfieldtubingtally.com.

For privacy questions, corrections, or deletion requests concerning Google Form or contact submissions, contact privacy@oilfieldtubingtally.com.

What to include in a bug report

Do not include customer, well, lease, personnel, operator, or job data in a support message or screenshot.

  • Device type, such as iPhone or iPad
  • iOS or iPadOS version, if known
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Whether the issue happens every time
  • Screenshots, if useful and safe to share

Important safety note

Oilfield Tubing Tally is a calculation aid. Results should be reviewed and verified against company procedures, field conditions, and qualified personnel judgment before use.
