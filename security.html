Security and Data Storage

Oilfield Tubing Tally does not currently use accounts, cloud sync, advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or third-party marketing SDKs.

Local device storage

Saved job data and app state may be stored locally on the user's device so work can continue later. Deleting the app or clearing app storage may delete locally stored information.

User-controlled sharing

Locally entered job data is not sent to the developer unless the user chooses to send it by email, message, screenshot, print/export, or another external sharing method.

Field data caution

Users should avoid sharing screenshots, reports, or exported information if they contain sensitive job details.
