Privacy Policy

Oilfield Tubing Tally is a calculation aid for tubing tally and volume estimates.

Information we collect

Oilfield Tubing Tally does not require an account. The app does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Information we collect

Job information entered into the app, such as well name, job date, setup values, joint lengths, saved jobs, and calculated results, is stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export, print, share, or otherwise transmit that information outside the app.

Local storage

The app may store saved jobs and app state locally on the user's device so the user can continue work later. Deleting the app or clearing app storage may delete locally stored information.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app does not use advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party marketing SDKs.

Support communication

If a user contacts support by email or another external method, the user may choose to provide contact information and details about their issue. That information is used only to respond to the request.

Data sharing

The app does not sell user data. The app does not share locally entered job data with the developer unless the user chooses to send it.

User choices

Users can avoid sharing job data by not exporting, printing, emailing, messaging, or otherwise transmitting reports outside the app. Users can delete saved jobs inside the app where the app provides that function.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact privacy@oilfieldtubingtally.com.
